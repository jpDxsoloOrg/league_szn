# Activity Feed

The Activity feed shows recent league happenings in one place: match results, title changes, season events, tournament updates, challenges, and promos. Find it in the **Home** section.

## What It Shows

Items in the feed can include:

- **Match results** — Completed matches with winners and losers. A draw shows as "Match completed".
- **Championship changes** — New champions crowned.
- **Season events** — Season start and end.
- **Tournament results** — New tournaments and tournament winners.
- **Challenges** — Challenges issued, accepted, declined, countered or withdrawn. Challenges that simply expire aren't listed.
- **Promos** — Promo posts from wrestlers. Call-outs and responses leave the feed 4 days after they're posted, unless staff have pinned them.

Promos and challenges involving a hidden player don't appear here. Match results and title changes stay, because they're league history.

## Order and Filtering

- **Order** — Items are ordered by time, newest first, so you see the latest activity at the top.
- **Type filter** — Use the tabs to show All, Matches, Championships, Seasons, Tournaments or Promos. Challenges have no tab of their own, so they show under All.
- **Load more** — The feed loads 20 items at a time.
