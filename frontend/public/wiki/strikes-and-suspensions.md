# Strikes and Suspensions

Strikes are how the league handles broken rules. Admins issue them, and each one comes with a reason you can read.

## How Strikes Work

| Strike | What happens | How long |
|--------|--------------|----------|
| **First strike** | A warning. You can still do everything. | Stays on your account for 7 days |
| **Second strike** | You can't sign up for 7 days. | Strikes stay on your account for 14 days from this strike |
| **Third strike** | Your account is deactivated. | Until an Admin reactivates it |

A third strike at any point in those 14 days deactivates your account.

If a warning ends with no second strike, your count goes back to zero. The same happens when the 14 days end. A later strike is then a fresh first strike.

## What a Suspension Stops

While you're blocked from signing up, you can't:

- Check in to a show as **Available** or **Tentative**
- Claim a slot on a match
- Issue, accept or counter a challenge
- Post a call-out promo

You can still set yourself as **Unavailable**, release a slot, decline a challenge, and use the rest of the site. Staff can still book you, and matches you're already booked in stay on the card. Pending challenges are cancelled when the block starts.

You stay in the standings and on rosters.

## If Your Account Is Deactivated

You can't log in, sign up for shows, or appear in standings and rosters. Your record and match history are kept. Only an Admin can reverse this, so contact one if you think it's a mistake.

## Seeing Your Status

If you have an active strike, a **Strikes** panel shows in the **Me** section and on your **Profile**. It lists each active strike with its date and reason, a live countdown to when your strikes fall off, the exact date, and how long any suspension has left. The suspension time also shows wherever you'd normally sign up.

With no active strikes there is no panel. Past strikes aren't shown.

## How Time Is Counted

A day here means 24 hours from the exact time of the strike, not a calendar day. A strike at 9:40 PM on a Monday ends its 7 days at 9:40 PM the next Monday, or an hour either side if the clocks change in between. An end can fall at any hour. All times show in your own time zone.
