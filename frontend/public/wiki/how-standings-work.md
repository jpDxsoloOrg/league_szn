# How Standings Work

The [Standings](standings) table is ranked by **league points**. Every result earns points, and a couple of bonuses reward the big moments. This page explains where your points come from and what decides the order when players are level.

## Points for a result

| Result | Points |
|--------|--------|
| Win | 3 |
| Draw | 1 |
| Loss | 0 |

It's the same in every kind of match. Winning a Fatal 4-Way is worth 3 points, just like a singles win. In a tag team match, everyone on the winning side gets 3 points. On a draw, everyone in the match gets 1.

## Bonus points

### Quality win: +1

You get 1 extra point for beating someone who was **above you in the table going into the match**, or who was **holding a title** at the time. It's about the upset as it happened: if the wrestler you beat slumps later, the bonus stays yours.

A match gives at most one quality win, even if you beat a champion who was also above you, or several higher-ranked wrestlers at once. In a tag team match, each member of the winning side is judged on their own.

### Streak bonus: +1

Every third win in a row adds 1 extra point: on your 3rd straight win, your 6th, your 9th and so on. A draw or a loss ends the run, and the count starts again from your next win. There is no penalty for a losing streak.

## Seeing your breakdown

Click or tap a player's points in the **Pts** column to see how they add up, for example:

> 12 = 9 from results + 2 quality wins + 1 streak bonus

The podium and the home page's **Top of the table** show points too, and a player's profile shows their points and where they rank, such as **#3 in Mid Card**.

## A worked example

Rhea is new in the Mid Card division and has 0 points.

1. **She beats Dante, who has 6 points.** Dante was above her, so that's 3 points + 1 quality win. Rhea now has **4**.
2. **She beats Kai, who has 2 points.** Kai was below her, so it's a plain win: 3 points. Rhea has **7**.
3. **She beats the Mid Card champion.** That's 3 points + 1 quality win for beating a champion + 1 streak bonus for her third straight win. Rhea has **12**.
4. **She draws with Dante.** A draw is worth 1 point and ends her streak. Rhea has **13**, and her next win starts a new run.

After step 3, her breakdown reads *12 = 9 from results + 2 quality wins + 1 streak bonus*.

## When players are level

If two or more players have the same points, these decide the order, one at a time:

1. **Strength of schedule.** How good the opponents you faced were, measured by their points per match. Tougher opponents put you higher.
2. **Head-to-head.** Points from the matches between the players who are level.
3. **Win %.** Wins ÷ (wins + losses + draws) × 100.
4. **Most wins.**
5. **Name**, in alphabetical order.

Players who are still level after step 4 share a rank, so a table can read 1, 2, 2, 4. Players who haven't had a match yet always come last.

Wrestlers who don't appear in the table, for example someone without a wrestler name, still count as opponents. Beating them earns the same points.

## Seasons, divisions and the All view

- **Season.** With a season picked, only matches from that season count. **All-Time** counts every match.
- **One division.** When you filter to a single division, like Heavyweight or Young Boys, each wrestler's points count only results since they joined that division. Someone promoted from Mid Card to Heavyweight starts the Heavyweight table at 0 points, at the bottom, and climbs from there. The promotion and relegation zones mark places among the wrestlers who have wrestled in that division. See [Promotion and Relegation](division-movement).
- **All divisions.** The **All** view and your career record on your profile keep every result, whatever division it was in. A move never changes them.

Each table works out quality wins with its own points. So a win over someone who was above you in the Jobber table counts there, even if they weren't above you in the All view.

## Standings and contender rankings

The standings and the [Contender Rankings](contenders) answer different questions:

- **Standings** add up points over the whole season, so steady results over time put you near the top.
- **Contender rankings** decide who's next in line for a title. They use a score built from win %, current streak, quality of wins and recent form, with newer matches counting for more. You need at least 3 matches to qualify, and staff can move a player up or down for storyline reasons.

So you can be high in the standings but further down a title's contender list, or the other way round, for example after a hot run of recent wins.
