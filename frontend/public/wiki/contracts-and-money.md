# Contracts and Money

The league has its own money now. You sign contracts, get paid for your matches, earn bonuses for winning and spend what you earn in the shop. Break the rules and you can be fined. This page explains how it all works.

The GMs switch the economy on for the league. Until they do, none of these pages show up. The GMs also set every amount, so this page talks about how things work, not exact prices.

## Where to find it

- **My Contract** shows your contract, your offers and your **Wallet**: your balance and every payment, bonus, fine and purchase.
- The **wallet card** on your dashboard shows your balance, what you earned this week and when the next payday is.
- **Free Agents** lists every wrestler without a contract.
- **Shop** is where you spend your money.

## Contract types

| Contract | How long it runs | How it pays |
|----------|------------------|-------------|
| **Tryout** | A few matches | Pay per match and a win bonus. No weekly salary. |
| **Match contract** | A set number of matches | Pay per match, a win bonus, and a weekly salary if the GM adds one. |
| **Season contract** | Until the current season ends | Usually a weekly salary as well as pay per match and a win bonus. |

Your contract also shows on your public profile, so everyone can see who's signed and who's a free agent. Your money stays private: only you and the GMs see your balance and your wallet.

## Offers

Contracts start as an offer from a GM. You'll get a notification, and the offer waits for you under **My Contract → Offers** with its terms, any note from the GM and how long you have left to answer.

- **Accept** to sign. If you had other open offers, they're withdrawn automatically.
- **Decline** if it's not for you. The GM can always make you a new offer.
- If you don't answer in time, the offer **expires**.
- If you already have a contract, you can't accept another one until it ends.

## How you get paid

- **Pay per match**: every time a result is recorded for a match you were in, win, lose or draw.
- **Win bonus**: on top of your match pay when you win. A draw is not a win.
- **Weekly salary**: paid every Sunday (league time) to everyone with an active contract that has a salary. A contract signed on a Sunday starts paying salary the following Sunday.

Every match you play counts toward your contract. When you've played all the matches on a tryout or match contract, it ends and you're a free agent again. A **season contract ends with the season**: when the GMs end the season, you get a notification and you're a free agent.

If a GM deletes a completed match, the pay and bonus for it are taken back, and the match no longer counts toward your contract.

## Free agents

No contract? You're a **free agent**. You don't get match pay or a salary, but you still earn the league's **free agent win bonus** every time you win. Free agents show up on the **Free Agents** page, which is where the GMs look when they want to sign someone, so keep winning.

## Fines and no-shows

GMs can fine you for breaking the rules, for example spamming cheesy moves or abusing pause. A fine always comes with a reason, and you'll see it in your wallet history.

If you're booked in a match and don't show up, a GM can mark you as a **no-show**. That costs you the league's no-show fine, and if it's marked before the result goes in, you don't get match pay for that match either. The match itself is handled as usual. If a GM made a mistake, they can undo the fine and you get the money back.

## Debt and the shop lock

Fines can push your balance below zero. When that happens you're **in debt**: you get a notification, and the shop stays locked until your balance is back to zero or more. You can still play and still get paid, so a few paydays or wins will get you out of it.

## The shop

Prices are set by the GMs, and an item only shows up if the GMs have put it in the shop.

| Item | What it does |
|------|--------------|
| **Injury shield** | Lowers your chance of getting injured for your next few matches. |
| **Rehab** | Takes time off an active injury. |
| **Stamina top-up** | Gives you some stamina back. |
| **Sim interference** | Boosts your rating in one of your scheduled matches when it's simulated. One per match. |
| **GM interference** | Pay the GM and ask for anything you like. See below. |

Every item works now. The injury shield, rehab and stamina top-up do their job while the GMs have stamina and injuries switched on; see [Stamina and Injuries](stamina-and-injuries) for how they fit in. Rehab needs an injury with at least two weeks left, and you can only have one injury shield active at a time.

Your purchases are private. Nobody else will see that you bought sim interference for a match. If a GM records that match by hand instead of simulating it, or the match is deleted, you get your money back.

## GM interference: a gamble

GM interference lets you pay up front and ask the GM for something: a title shot, a run-in, a bad night for your rival. Whatever you can dream up.

It's a gamble. The GM decides what happens, and **the GM may keep your money and do nothing**. You have to tick a box saying you understand that before you can pay. When the GM decides, you get a notification with one of these outcomes:

| Outcome | What it means |
|---------|---------------|
| **Delivered** | The GM did what you asked. |
| **Partly delivered** | The GM did some of it. |
| **Money taken** | The GM kept your money and did nothing. Better luck next time. |
| **Refunded** | The GM turned it down and gave your money back. |

You can only have one request waiting at a time. Your requests and their outcomes are listed in the shop under **My requests**.
