# My Profile

*This page is for signed-in wrestlers. Find it under **Me**.*

My Profile is where you manage your wrestler and see your league history.

## Entering your wrestler name

If you haven't entered a wrestler name, a notice at the top says you're hidden from the site. Click **Enter wrestler name**, type any name and save. You appear on the site straight away.

## Editing your profile

Click **Edit profile** to change:

- Your wrestler name and an alternate wrestler
- Your player name and PSN ID
- Your alignment: Face, Neutral or Heel
- Your bio
- Up to 5 signature moves and 5 finishers
- Your main and alternate overalls (60 to 99)
- Your wrestler image

Add at least one signature and one finisher so commentary can call your matches. If anything important is missing, the site reminds you after you sign in. **Remind me later** puts that off until your next visit.

## Overview

The Overview tab shows your all-time record and win percentage, your moveset, your overalls and your record in each season.

## Requests

The Requests tab is where you pitch ideas to the GMs. Pick a type (Storyline, Backstage attack or Rivalry), choose the target players, describe your idea and click **Submit request**. **My requests** lists what you've sent and each request's status. A GM may add a note when they answer.

## Stats

When the league has statistics turned on, the Stats tab shows your full numbers. See [Statistics](statistics).

## Your public profile

Everyone can see a public profile for each wrestler, opened by clicking a name in the [Standings](standings). It shows titles held, recent form, recent matches, rivalries, season records and moveset. On another wrestler's profile, signed-in wrestlers get a **Challenge** button that starts a [call-out](challenges).
