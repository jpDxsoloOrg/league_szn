# Managing Championships

The **Championships** page allows you to create and manage titles.

## Creating a Championship

1. Go to **Championships** and click **Create Championship**
2. Fill in the championship details:
   - **Championship Name** — e.g., "World Heavyweight Championship"
   - **Type** — Singles or Tag Team
   - **Division** (optional) — Locks the title and its contender rankings to one division. Leave it as **No Division (Open to all)** otherwise
   - **Championship Belt Image** (optional) — Upload an image of the belt
3. Click **Create Championship** to save

## Editing a Championship

1. Find the championship in the table
2. Click **Edit**
3. Update the details
4. Click **Update Championship** to confirm changes

## Assigning or Vacating a Champion

- **Assign Champion** crowns a champion directly, without a match. Any current reign is closed and the new reign is added to the title history. A tag title needs two different players.
- **Vacate** strips the current champion of the title.

## Deactivating a Championship

**Deactivate** takes a title out of use for new matches and challenges, but keeps its history visible. **Activate** brings it back. Inactive titles stay in this table and show an Inactive badge.

## Deleting a Championship

1. Find the championship in the table
2. Click **Delete**
3. Confirm the action in the dialog

**Warning:** Deleting a championship will also permanently delete all championship history (past reigns, dates, etc.). Use **Deactivate** if you want to keep the history.

## How Championships Work

- When you schedule a championship match, select the title at stake. If the title is locked to a division, everyone in the match must be in that division
- When the result is recorded, the winner becomes champion. If the champion wins, it counts as a successful defense
- A draw leaves the title where it is
- Championship history is tracked automatically with dates, reign lengths and defenses
- Each active title has its own #1 contender rankings; see [Contender Rankings](admin-contender-config)
