# Events & PPV

View upcoming and past events, including PPVs, weekly shows, and special events. Find them in the **Events** section.

## Browsing Events

The **Calendar** tab leads with the event that's live, or the next one coming up. Below that you'll find events in progress, upcoming events, planned shows and recent results. On a computer there's also a month calendar. On a phone, use the **Upcoming** and **Past** tabs instead.

Use the type filter to find specific events:

- **PPV** — Major events like WrestleMania, SummerSlam, Royal Rumble
- **Weekly** — Regular weekly shows like Raw, SmackDown, NXT
- **Special** — One-off special events
- **House Show** — Non-televised exhibition events

A **planned** show is one on the league's schedule that doesn't have a card yet. Cancelled events are marked as cancelled. Recent results show 10 events at a time, with **Show more** for older ones.

## Event Details

Click on any event to see its full details:

- The match card, with any pre-show matches in their own section
- Championship matches highlighted with a special badge
- Event status, date, venue and description
- Results for completed matches, including draws
- **View Full Results** once at least one match is finished
- **Commentary View**, which gives each match a page of wrestler details for the people calling the show

## Getting Booked

Signed-in wrestlers see **Will you be there?** on the event page. Choose **Available**, **Tentative** or **Unavailable**. To get booked, click **Available**. You can change your answer or use **Clear my response** until the event is completed or cancelled, or until staff lock sign-ups.

Some matches have open spots. Signed-in wrestlers can claim a spot and release it again, with one spot per event.

While a [strike](strikes-and-suspensions) blocks you from signing up, **Available**, **Tentative** and claiming a spot are off, and the page shows when the block lifts. **Unavailable** stays on.

## Matches

The **Matches** tab lists every match. Search by name, choose All, Completed or Scheduled, or use **Filters** for player, match type, stipulation, championship, season and dates. **Details** opens a match, and that's where you can [rate it](rate-matches) once it's completed. The list shows 50 matches at a time, with **Show more** at the bottom.

## How Cards Are Built

Moderators and Admins build cards on the event page. **Add Match** books a match onto the card, and the **Move up** and **Move down** arrows change the running order while the event is upcoming or in progress. Pre-show and main card matches are ordered separately. There's no separate card builder.
