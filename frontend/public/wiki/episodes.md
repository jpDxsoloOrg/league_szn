# Episodes

*Anyone can watch episodes and highlights. You don't need to sign in.*

The **Episodes** page in the **Events** section is where the league's shows live. It has two tabs: **Full Episodes** and **Highlights**. The old Highlights page now opens the Highlights tab here.

## Full Episodes

Full episodes are the league's shows on YouTube. Each one shows as a card with its thumbnail, title, air date and, when it has one, the event it belongs to. Newest episodes come first. Click a card to open the episode page.

## Watching an episode

On the episode page, click the thumbnail to start the video. It plays right on the page. The YouTube player only loads once you click, so the page stays quick until you want to watch.

Playing an episode here counts as a view on YouTube, so you support the league's channel either way. **Watch on YouTube** under the player opens the video on youtube.com in a new tab, for example to watch on your TV or to leave a comment.

If a video won't play on the site, **Watch on YouTube** always works.

## The event card

When an episode belongs to a league event, the episode page shows **On the card**: the event's name, its type and status, and every match with its participants. Winners are highlighted once results are in. Click the event name to open the event, or **View full results** for the full results.

It works the other way round too: the page of an event with an episode shows **Watch the episode**. See [Events & PPV](events).

## Highlights

The **Highlights** tab has the clips that are hosted on the site: match highlights, promos and more. Use the tabs at the top to show a single category. Clips that belong to an episode show **From episode** with a link to it.

An episode page lists its own clips under **Highlights from this episode**.

## Live stream

When the league streams a show live on Twitch, the [Dashboard](dashboard) shows a **Live** card at the top. The GMs switch it on when the stream starts, so the card only appears while the show is on. Click the player to watch on the site, or **Watch on Twitch** to open the stream on twitch.tv in a new tab, where you'll also find the chat.

If the GMs forget to switch the card off, it disappears by itself 8 hours after the stream started.
