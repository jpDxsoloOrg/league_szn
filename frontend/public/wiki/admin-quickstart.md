# Admin Quickstart Guide

New admin? Follow these steps to set up your league from scratch. Open **Admin** from the top bar, or from **Me** on a phone. Steps 1 and the sample data at the end need a full Admin; a Moderator can do the rest.

## Steps

1. **Check Users** — Go to **User Management**. Everyone who signs up gets the **Wrestler** role and a player record automatically, so you only need this page to add Moderators and Admins, or to give someone a wrestler or division.

2. **Create Divisions** — Go to **Divisions** and create the divisions your league uses. Divisions group players and can lock a title and its contender rankings to one division.

3. **Manage Players** — Go to **Manage Players** to edit player details, upload images, assign divisions and remove players. Staff don't add players here; players appear when people sign up. A player with no wrestler name is hidden until they enter one, so tick **Show hidden** to find them.

4. **Create a Season** — Go to **Seasons** and create your first season. Only one season can be active at a time. Contender rankings need an active season.

5. **Create Championships** — Go to **Championships** and create your titles (e.g., World Heavyweight, Intercontinental, Tag Team). Upload belt images for a polished look.

6. **Set Match Config** — Go to **Match Config** and define your match types and stipulations, so the options are ready when you schedule.

7. **Create an Event** — Go to the **Events** section and click **Create Event**. Pick the type and set the name, date and venue. The new event's page opens when you save.

8. **Build the Match Card** — On the event page, click **Add Match** to schedule matches straight onto the card. Use **Move up** and **Move down** to set the running order, and **Edit** on a match to change its card position.

9. **Record Results** — After a match is played, click **Record Result** on it on the event page. Standalone matches are under **Standalone Matches**. Pick the winner or tick **Draw**, then save. Standings, championships and tournament brackets update automatically.

10. **Check Contenders** — Contender rankings recalculate after each result and every 6 hours. Use **Contender Config** if you want to run them by hand, and **Contender Overrides** for storyline changes.

## Quick Demo Mode

**Generate Sample Data** in the **Danger Zone** fills the league with sample players, divisions, championships, matches, tournaments and a season. It only works on the test site; on the live league site it is switched off. See [Data Management](admin-data-management).
