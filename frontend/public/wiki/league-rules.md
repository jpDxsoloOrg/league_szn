# 🚨 WWE 2K League Rules 🚨

This league is all about **having fun, creating entertaining matches, and enjoying the game**. Play fair, keep it realistic, and most importantly — have fun!

## Code of Conduct

- **Keep created wrestlers believable.** Please do not make weird, unrealistic, or overly exaggerated created wrestlers — keep your characters believable and fitting for a WWE-style roster.
- **No cheesy or spammy move sets.** Move sets designed solely to win or sweat out your opponent have no place here. We're here to have competitive and fun matches, not treat every fight like it's the WWE 2K World Championship.
- **Play fair.** No cheating, exploiting glitches, or intentionally abusing game mechanics.

## Enforcement

If you are caught cheating, exploiting glitches, intentionally abusing game mechanics, or otherwise breaking the league rules, **your fate will be left up to the League Board**.

Punishments can range from:

1. A warning
2. Suspension
3. Not being booked for a period of time
4. In more extreme cases, being **kicked from the league entirely**

## Divisions & Rating Caps

| Division | Max Overall Rating |
|----------|:------------------:|
| Heavyweight | **92** |
| Mid-Card | **88** |
| Jobber | **80** |

### Heavyweight Division

The Heavyweight Division is reserved for some of the league's highest-rated wrestlers. Wrestlers in this division can have an overall rating of up to **92**, giving them the opportunity to compete in the biggest matches and storylines.

### Mid-Card Division

The Mid-Card Division is for wrestlers who are building their way toward the top. Created wrestlers in this division can have an overall rating of up to **88**, keeping them competitive while giving them room to grow.

### Jobber Division

The Jobber Division is designed for wrestlers who are starting out or playing a supporting role on the roster. Created wrestlers in this division can have an overall rating of up to **80**, while still being able to compete, improve, and make an impact on the league.
