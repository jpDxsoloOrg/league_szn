# Tournaments Page

Follow tournament brackets and standings for ongoing and completed tournaments. Find them in the **Events** section.

## Tournament Types

The league supports two tournament formats:

### Single Elimination

- Traditional bracket-style tournament
- If you lose, you're eliminated
- Winners advance to the next round as soon as the result is recorded
- These matches can't end in a draw
- Last person standing wins the tournament

### Round Robin (G1 Climax Style)

- Every participant faces every other participant
- Points awarded: Win = 2 pts, Draw = 1 pt, Loss = 0 pts
- Player with the most points at the end wins
- A tie goes to the player with more wins, then to the player with more wins in matches between the tied players

## Tournament Information

Each tournament card shows:

- Tournament name and type
- Status (Upcoming, In Progress, or Completed)
- Number of participants
- The winner, once there is one

Click **View Details** to see the bracket or standings and the full list of participants.
