# Rate Matches

After a Match has been marked Completed by a GM, signed-in users can rate it from 0.5 to 5 stars.

## How it works

- Open a completed match: **Events**, then pick an event, or use the **Matches** tab and click **Details**.
- Hover the star strip to preview a rating, then click to commit. On a phone, tap the star.
- Each user can rate a match **once**. After you rate, the widget locks and shows your rating.
- The match's overall star rating is the average of all submitted user ratings, rounded to the nearest half star.

## Why it matters

Ratings decide the [Best Matches](statistics) list. When a match belongs to a rivalry, every rating you give nudges the rivalry's **heat**. Matches rated above 2.5 stars make a rivalry hotter; matches rated below 2.5 cool it down.

See also: [How rivalry heat works](rivalry-heat-explained).
