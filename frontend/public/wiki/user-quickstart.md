# User Quickstart

New here? Use this short path to learn the site in 5 minutes.

## 1) Start at Home

Open the [Dashboard](dashboard) to see:
- the live or next event
- current champions
- recent results
- the top of the table
- season progress and quick stats

## 2) Check where you stand

Go to **Rankings**, then [Standings](standings):
- use **Season** to switch between all-time and season standings
- use the **Division** filter and **Alignment** to narrow the list

## 3) Follow active competition

- [Events](events): the calendar, match cards and results
- [Titles](championships): current champions and title history
- [Tournaments](tournaments): brackets and round-robin standings
- [Activity](activity): latest league actions in one feed

## 4) Enter your wrestler name

After you sign in, the site asks for your wrestler name. You stay hidden from other players until you enter one, so do this first. See [Getting Started](getting-started).

## 5) Get involved

Once you have a wrestler name, these are all under **Me** or **Storylines**:
- [My Profile](profile): your record, moves and requests
- Event pages: click **Available** so the GMs can book you ([Events](events))
- [Call-outs](challenges): challenge another wrestler
- [Promos](promos): have your say
- [Rate matches](rate-matches): give completed matches a star rating

## 6) Need help fast?

- Read the [League Rules](league-rules)
- Browse [FAQs](faqs)
- Use [Tips](tips)
- If you are league staff, open the [Admin Quickstart](admin-quickstart)
