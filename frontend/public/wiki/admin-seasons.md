# Managing Seasons

The **Seasons** page allows you to organize the league into distinct competitive periods.

## Creating a Season

1. Go to **Seasons** and click **Create New Season**
2. Fill in the season details:
   - **Season Name** — e.g., "Season 1", "Summer 2025"
   - **Start Date** — When the season begins
   - **End Date** (optional) — When the season ends
3. Click **Create Season** to save

## Important: One Active Season Rule

Only one season can be active at a time. You must end the current season before creating a new one.

## Ending a Season

1. Find the active season in the **Active Season** banner
2. Click **End Season**
3. Confirm the action in the dialog
4. The season's status changes to Completed. If it had no end date, today's date is recorded

An ended season's standings are final. A scheduled match that still belongs to it can't get a result until you edit the match and choose a different season. Contender rankings stop until a new season is active.

## Deleting a Season

1. Find the season in the **All Seasons** table
2. Click **Delete**
3. Confirm the action in the dialog

You can't delete the active season; end it first.

**Warning:** Deleting a season also permanently deletes all standings and awards for that season. This action cannot be undone.

## How Seasons Work

- New matches start on the active season when scheduled. Choose **No Season (Exhibition)** to keep a match out of season standings
- Season-specific standings are tracked separately from all-time standings
- When a match result is recorded, both all-time and season standings update automatically
- Completed seasons keep their records and can be viewed at any time under **Rankings → Standings**
