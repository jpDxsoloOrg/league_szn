# Recording Results

There is no separate results page any more. You record a result on the match itself:

- **Matches on an event card**: open the event from the **Events** section and click **Record Result** on the match
- **Standalone matches**: go to **Admin → Standalone Matches** and click **Record Result** there

## Recording a Match Result

1. Click **Record Result** on a scheduled match
2. Choose the outcome:
   - **Tag team matches** — Select the winning team
   - **Other matches** — Select the winner or winners; everyone else takes the loss
   - **Draw** — Tick **Draw**, and every participant gets a draw
3. Click **Record Result** to save

## What Happens When You Record a Result

- Player win/loss/draw records update, both all-time and for the match's season
- League standings update
- If it's a championship match, the title goes to the winner, or the champion's defense count goes up if they retain
- If it's a tournament match, brackets or round robin standings update
- The first result on an event moves it to **In Progress**, and the last one moves it to **Completed**
- Contender rankings recalculate in the background

## Draws

- A draw adds one draw to every participant
- A draw never changes a championship, so the champion keeps the title
- In a round robin tournament each player gets 1 point
- A single elimination match can't end in a draw; the site refuses it

## When a Result Is Refused

- **The match's season has ended.** Ended seasons are final. Edit the match and choose a different season first.
- **A participant has been removed.** Edit the match participants first.
- **The match still has open signup spots.** Fill every spot first.

## Fixing a Mistake

Results can't be edited. On the event page, **Delete** the completed match; its stats, title change or tournament progress are rolled back. Then schedule the match again and record the right result. The site tells you if something later in a title's history or a bracket stops the rollback.

Completed standalone matches don't appear under **Standalone Matches**, so they can't be deleted from that page.
