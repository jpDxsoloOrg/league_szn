# Install the App

League SZN can live on your home screen like an app. It opens full screen, without the browser's address bar, and it is the same site with the same account. There is nothing to download from an app store.

## Android (Chrome)

1. Open the site in **Chrome**.
2. Open the **Me** tab (it is labelled **Sign In** if you aren't signed in) and tap **Install app**. If you don't see it, open Chrome's **⋮** menu and tap **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.
3. Confirm with **Install**.

The League SZN icon appears on your home screen and in your app list.

## iPhone and iPad (Safari)

1. Open the site in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up). On iPhone it is at the bottom of the screen, on iPad at the top. On newer iOS versions Share may sit inside the **•••** menu: tap **•••** first, then **Share**.
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Tap **Add**.

On iOS 16.4 and later, Chrome and Edge for iPhone have the same **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen** option.

## Computer

**Chrome or Microsoft Edge**

1. Open the site in **Chrome** or **Microsoft Edge**.
2. Click the install icon at the right of the address bar (a screen with a down arrow). If you are signed in, you can also go to **Me** and click **Install app**.
3. Click **Install**.

**Safari on a Mac (Safari 17 or later)**

1. Open the site in **Safari**.
2. Choose **File** > **Add to Dock** (or click **Share**, then **Add to Dock**).
3. Click **Add**.

The app opens in its own window and you can pin it to your taskbar or dock. Firefox on a computer can't install it; use the site in a tab as usual.

## Signing in

On Android and on a computer you stay signed in. On iPhone and iPad the home-screen app keeps its own storage, separate from Safari, so you sign in once more after adding it.

## Updates

Updates are automatic. The app always loads the latest version of the site when it opens. If something looks out of date, close the app fully and open it again.

## It needs the internet

Standings, shows, results and everything else come live from the league, so the app needs a connection. Without one it shows a short **You're offline** page with a **Retry** button.

## Removing it

Remove it like any other app: press and hold the icon, then choose **Remove** or **Uninstall**. On a computer, open the app's **⋮** menu and choose **Uninstall**.
