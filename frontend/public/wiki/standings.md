# Standings Page

The Standings page shows the league table. Find it in the **Rankings** section.

## Choosing what to see

The page opens on the active season and the Heavyweight division, when the league has them. You can change that with:

- **Season** — A single season, or All-Time.
- **Alignment** — Face, Neutral or Heel.
- **Division** — One division, or All. **No Division** lists players without one.

## What You Can See

The top three appear on a podium once at least three players have had a match. Everyone else is listed underneath.

| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Rank** | Player's position in the current list |
| **Player** | The player's name, with their wrestler underneath |
| **Record** | Wins, losses and draws |
| **Win %** | Win percentage calculated from their record |
| **Last 5** | Their last five results |
| **Streak** | Current run of results, such as W3, L2 or D1 |

On a computer, click a column heading to sort the table, or hover over a name to see the player's division, overall, PSN ID, alignment and last result. Click a name to open the player's profile. The list shows 50 rows at a time, and **Show more** loads the next 50.

Only players with a wrestler name appear here. See [Getting Started](getting-started).

## How Rankings Work

Players are ordered by most wins, then by fewest losses. Players who haven't had a match in the selected season come last. Draws count in the record and in Win %, but they don't decide the order.

**Formula:** Win % = Wins ÷ (Wins + Losses + Draws) × 100
