# Contender Rankings

The Contenders page shows the official #1 contender rankings for each championship. Find it in the **Rankings** section, and use the dropdown to pick a title.

## What Are Contender Rankings?

Contender rankings are calculated automatically from completed matches in the active season. If no season is active, they aren't updated. Each championship has its own rankings, showing who has earned the right to challenge for the title.

- The current champion is shown at the top and isn't ranked.
- You need at least 3 matches in the season to qualify, and a title lists up to 10 contenders. Staff can place a player on the list without them.
- A title tied to a division normally ranks only players in that division.
- Hidden players and players removed by staff aren't ranked.

## When Rankings Update

Rankings recalculate after each recorded result and every 6 hours. The time of the latest run is shown at the bottom of the page.

## Reading the Rankings

| Label | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Rank** | The player's contender position (#1 is next in line for a title shot) |
| **Score** | The calculated ranking score |
| **Win %** | The player's win percentage this season |
| **Streak** | Current winning or losing streak, such as W3 or L2 |

An arrow beside the rank shows how far a player has moved since their rank last changed. **NEW** marks a player who has just entered the rankings.

## How the Score Works

| Part | Weight |
|------|--------|
| Win percentage | 40% |
| Current streak | 20% |
| Quality of wins (how good the opponents you beat are) | 25% |
| Recent results (newer matches count for more) | 15% |

A draw counts as a match played but not as a win, and it ends a streak.

## Staff Overrides

Staff can move a player for storyline or disciplinary reasons. These players carry a **Bumped to #1** or **Sent to Bottom** badge, along with the rank they earned from results.
