# Typical Weekly Workflow

A suggested order for running your league week to week.

## 1. Create an Event

In the **Events** section, click **Create Event** and set up the weekly show or PPV with its name, date and venue.

## 2. Schedule Matches

Open the event and click **Add Match** to schedule matches straight onto the card, including championship and tournament bouts.

## 3. Arrange the Card

Still on the event page, use **Move up** and **Move down** to set the running order, and **Edit** on a match to set its card position (pre-show, opener, midcard, co-main, main event).

## 4. Record Results

After the matches are played, click **Record Result** on each match on the event page. Pick the winner, or tick **Draw**. Matches that aren't on an event are under **Standalone Matches**.

## 5. Check Contenders

There's nothing you have to do here. Contender rankings recalculate after each result and again every 6 hours. Use **Contender Overrides** for storyline changes.

## 6. Review Standings

Check the updated standings and contender rankings under **Rankings**. Long lists show the first rows only, so use **Show more** to see the rest.

## 7. Review call-outs and promos

Under **Promos**, schedule matches from accepted challenges (on the **Call-outs** tab) or from call-out promos when you're ready. Clear hidden promos as needed. A full Admin can also use **Clear Resolved** on the **Call-outs** tab.

Unanswered challenges expire by themselves after 4 days, and call-out and response promos leave the public feeds after 4 days, so you only need to act on the ones you want to book.

## 8. Look for hidden players

In **Manage Players**, tick **Show hidden** to see who still has no wrestler name. They stay off public pages and out of booking until they enter a name or staff assign a wrestler.
