# Admin: Season Awards

Use **Season Awards** to review auto-generated awards and add your own custom awards for a selected season. Members see them under **Rankings → Awards**.

## How it works

- Select a season first.
- The page shows:
  - **Auto Awards**, calculated from season data: MVP, Longest Win Streak, Iron Man, Best Win Percentage and Most Championship Defenses
  - **Custom Awards**, created by staff

## Add a custom award

1. Pick a season.
2. Click **Create Award**.
3. Enter award name, player, and optional description.
4. Save and check it appears in the custom awards list.

## Delete a custom award

- Click **Delete** in the custom awards table.
- Confirm deletion when prompted.

Auto awards are calculated by the site and can't be deleted from this page. Deleting a season also deletes its custom awards.
