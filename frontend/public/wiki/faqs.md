# FAQs

Common questions about the WWE 2K League site.

## Finding your way around

**Where did the sidebar and the Basic/Advanced menu go?**  
They're gone, along with the More sheet. The site now has five sections: Home, Events, Rankings, Storylines and Me. League staff also get Admin. See [Getting Started](getting-started).

**Where is Help on a phone?**  
Open **Me**. Help, League Rules and the language control are all there.

**Why does a long list stop part way down?**  
Standings and the Matches tab show 50 rows at a time, and past events on the Calendar show 10. Use **Show more** at the bottom to see the next batch.

## Your account

**Why can't other players see me?**  
You're hidden until you enter a wrestler name. Enter one from the prompt, the Me page or [My Profile](profile). You appear as soon as you save.

**What can't I do while I'm hidden?**  
You can't cut promos, issue or counter call-outs, find a match or claim a spot on a card. You can still sign in, edit your profile and answer a call-out with Accept or Decline.

**Why is someone missing from the standings?**  
Players with no wrestler name are hidden from public pages. Staff can still find them in Manage Players with **Show hidden**.

**What does "Former member" mean?**  
A player removed by staff leaves the roster, but their results stay in the history. Past records keep the name on file. If no name can be found, the record shows **Former member**.

## Standings and rankings

**How are standings ordered?**  
Most wins first, then fewest losses. Players who haven't had a match go to the bottom. Draws count in your record and your win percentage, but they don't decide the order.

**What's the difference between All-Time and Season standings?**  
All-Time shows every match ever recorded. Season standings show only matches that are part of the selected season. Use the Season dropdown on the Standings page to switch.

**How does a draw count?**  
It goes in your record as a draw for everyone in the match. It isn't a win or a loss. It ends a winning or losing streak, a title can't change hands on a draw, and a round-robin draw is worth 1 point.

**How often do contender rankings update?**  
After each recorded result and every 6 hours. Staff can also recalculate them by hand. See [Contender Rankings](contenders).

## Divisions

**Can I change division?**  
Staff assign divisions. Division transfers are turned off at the moment.

## Championships

**What does "Vacant" mean?**  
A title is vacant when nobody currently holds it.

**Where do I see past champions?**  
Go to **Rankings**, then **Titles**, and click a title. Its history lists each champion, the dates won and lost, days held and defenses.

## Events and matches

**Where did the Matches page go?**  
It's the **Matches** tab in the Events section. You can search and filter every match there. To see a whole show, open an event from the **Calendar** tab.

**How do I get booked?**  
Open the event page and click **Available**. See [Events](events).

**Where did the Match Card Builder go?**  
It's gone. Staff now build and reorder a card on the event's own page.

## Call-outs and promos

**How long do I have to answer a challenge?**  
4 days. After that it shows as Expired and can't be answered.

**Why did my call-out disappear from the promo feed?**  
Call-outs and responses leave public feeds 4 days after they are posted, unless a staff member has pinned them. A direct link still works. Other promo types stay in the feed.

## Tournaments

**What's single-elimination?**  
You're out after one loss. The bracket advances until one winner remains. These matches can't end in a draw.

**What's round-robin?**  
Everyone faces everyone. A win is worth 2 points and a draw is worth 1. Most points wins the tournament.

## League staff

**What's the difference between a Moderator and an Admin?**  
Both are league staff, also called GMs. Moderators can run nearly everything: events, results, rosters, titles, seasons, promos and rivalries. Only Admins can open User Management, Heat Tunables, Feature Management and the Danger Zone.
