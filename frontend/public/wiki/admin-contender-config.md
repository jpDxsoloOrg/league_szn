# Contender Rankings

Each active championship has its own #1 contender rankings, shown under **Rankings → Contenders**. They build and update on their own, and staff can change a position with an override.

## How Contender Rankings Work

- Rankings use completed matches in the active season. With no active season, nothing is calculated
- A player's score combines win percentage, current streak, quality of wins and how recent their matches are
- A player needs at least 3 matches to appear, and each title lists up to 10 contenders
- The current champion is left out, and so are removed players
- A title locked to a division (set under **Championships**) only ranks players in that division

## When Rankings Update

There's nothing to run. Rankings recalculate for all active titles:

- After every match result
- Every 6 hours

## Contender Overrides

Use **Contender Overrides** to change a position for storyline or disciplinary reasons:

1. Select a championship and a player
2. Choose **Bump to #1 Contender** or **Send to Bottom**
3. Enter a reason, and optionally a date for it to expire
4. Click **Apply Override**

Overrides work for anyone who can contend for the title, whatever their record:

- **Bump to #1 Contender** puts the player at #1 even with fewer than 3 matches, no matches at all, or a score outside the top 10. Their row shows their real stats for the season, and the lowest ranked player drops off so the list stays at 10
- **Send to Bottom** puts the player last on the list. A player who isn't on the list stays off it, and the page tells you nothing moved
- With several overrides on one title, the newest bump is #1 with older bumps below it, and the newest Send to Bottom is last

The player must be on the roster with a wrestler name, and on a title locked to a division they must be in it. The current champion can't be overridden.

Applying an override recalculates that title straight away, and the page tells you the position the player reached, or why they aren't on the list. Removing one recalculates the title too. The **Position** column shows where each active override stands now. Overrides stay in place through every recalculation until they expire or you click **Remove Override**. An override ends by itself if the player becomes champion.
