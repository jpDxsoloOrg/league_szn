# Promos

*Anyone can read promos. Cutting and reacting are for signed-in wrestlers. Your league may enable or disable this feature.*

Cut promos to build storylines, call out opponents, or respond to other wrestlers. Promos appear in the feed and can be linked to challenges.

## Promo feed

Open **Storylines**, then **Promos**. Pinned promos appear at the top, and the rest are grouped by date. Use the tabs to show All, Call-Outs, Championship, Pre-Match (this covers pre- and post-match promos) or Mentions, which lists promos aimed at you. **Hide Read** and **Mark all as read** help you keep up.

Responses don't appear in the main feed. To read them, open the promo with **View Thread**. Its thread shows the original plus every reply.

## How long promos stay up

Call-outs and responses leave public feeds 4 days after they're posted, unless staff have pinned them. They aren't deleted: a direct link still opens them, and they still count towards [rivalry heat](rivalry-heat-explained). Other promo types stay in the feed.

## Creating a promo

Click **Cut a Promo** to open the editor. A title is optional. The body must be 50 to 2,000 characters. Use **@WrestlerName** to highlight another wrestler's name.

Choose a type:

- **Open Mic** — Speak your mind on any topic.
- **Call-Out** — Challenge another wrestler directly. This issues a [challenge](challenges).
- **Response** — Reply to another wrestler's promo. Pick the wrestler, then their promo.
- **Pre-Match** and **Post-Match** — Talk about a match.
- **Championship** — Address a title situation.
- **Return** — Announce your return to the league.
- **Rivalry** — Advance one of your [rivalries](rivalries).

If you pick a target wrestler, they get a notification.

You need a wrestler name to cut a promo. Promos by hidden players don't appear in public feeds.

## Reacting to promos

Wrestlers can leave one reaction on a promo: Fire, Mic, Trash, Mind-Blown or Clap. Click your reaction again to take it back, or pick another to swap it. Everyone can see the counts. On promos that feed a rivalry, Fire and Trash also move its heat.

## Call-outs and challenges

A call-out promo issues a challenge. The challenged wrestler can accept, decline, or counter it. Accepting doesn't book the match. Once staff schedule it, it appears on the event card and in your match history.
