# Admin: Promos

The **Promos** page (**Admin → Promos**) has two tabs. **Promos** manages the promo feed, and **Call-outs** moderates challenges between players (see [Challenges](admin-challenges)).

## Viewing and filtering

The list shows every promo, including hidden and archived ones. Filter by type (open mic, call-out, response, and so on) and by status (pinned, hidden, archived).

## Archived Call-outs and Responses

Promos of the call-out and response types leave the public feeds 4 days after they are posted. Nothing is deleted. They show an **Archived** badge here, they still open from a direct link, and they still count towards rivalry heat.

A pinned promo doesn't age out. Other promo types stay in the feed.

## Pin / Unpin

Pinned promos appear at the top of the public feed.

## Hide

Hidden promos are removed from the public feed until you click **Unhide**. Scheduling a match from a call-out promo hides it automatically.

## Schedule Match (call-out promos)

For call-out promos that name a target, and tag call-outs with a challenge, click **Schedule Match** to open Schedule Match with both players or teams pre-filled, along with the match type, stipulation and title from the promo's challenge. If the challenge was countered, the counter's terms are used. The match stores a link to the promo and marks its challenge as scheduled, so the same call-out can't be booked twice. If its match was deleted, use this button to book it again.

## Deleting promos

Use the **Delete** button on a row to remove a promo.

## Clear hidden promos

The **Clear hidden promos** button permanently deletes all hidden promos after you confirm. Archived promos aren't affected unless they are also hidden.

Moderators and Admins can use everything on this tab.
