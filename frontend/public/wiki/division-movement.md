# Promotion and Relegation

Getting called up out of Young Boys is only the start. The main roster is a ladder: some divisions sit higher than others, and the GMs move wrestlers up and down it as the seasons go by. This page explains how that works, what the GMs look at, and what it means for you.

## The division ladder

Every main-roster division can have a **tier**. Tier 1 is the top. Divisions on the same tier are equals. In this league the ladder runs **Heavyweight** at the top, then **Mid Card**, then **Jobber**. **Young Boys** and any other developmental division always sit below every tier.

- A **promotion** moves you up one rung, for example from Mid Card to Heavyweight.
- A **relegation** moves you down one rung, for example from Mid Card to Jobber.
- A division without a tier isn't part of the ladder, so nobody gets promoted or relegated out of it.

## Your promotion meter

If there is a division above yours, your profile shows a gold **promotion meter** under **Division Movement**. It works like the call-up bar: each goal fills a share of the meter, and the meter moves a little as you get closer to a goal, not only when you finish it.

The default promotion goals are:

| Goal | What it means |
|------|---------------|
| **Win 60% of your last 8 matches** | Your recent form. It starts counting once you have 8 matches since your last move. |
| **Average at least 3.25 stars** | The average user star rating of your matches. Ask people to rate them! |
| **Be a top-3 contender** | Rank in the top 3 contenders for any title that belongs to your division. |
| **Earn a Match of the Night** | Steal the show once. |

The GMs can change the goals for each division, so your meter may show slightly different ones.

Everyone can see your meter's overall percentage. Only you and the GMs see the goal-by-goal checklist.

## Promotion watch

When a GM thinks you're getting close, they can put you on **promotion watch**. You'll get a notification, a banner appears on your profile, your name runs on the League Wire under **Promotion watch**, and it shows up in the activity feed. It's the GMs telling the whole league that you're one to watch.

## Staying sharp

The GMs also keep an eye on wrestlers who are struggling, so the ladder stays fair for everyone. What they look at:

- losing most of your recent matches (70% or more of your last 8)
- going 30 days without a match
- a very low star average (2 stars or less over at least 5 rated matches)

The GMs can also look at two more things for a division:

- **losses**: how many matches you've lost since your last move
- **winless run**: how many matches in a row you've gone without a win (draws count as no win)

You will never see a relegation meter or a relegation warning about yourself. Instead, if one of these applies, your profile shows a friendly **Stay sharp** card with a tip or two, like chasing a few more wins or asking for a match. Follow the tips and you're in good shape.

### Storyline losses never count against you

Losing a big storyline match is part of the job, and you will never be punished for it. If a match belongs to an **active rivalry that is Hot or Scorching**, a loss in that match is simply ignored when the GMs look at struggling form. It doesn't count as a loss, it doesn't add to your losses or a winless run, and it doesn't extend a losing streak. So if you're putting someone over in a red-hot feud, lose with pride.

See [How Rivalry Heat Works](rivalry-heat-explained) for how a rivalry heats up.

## Standings zones

Filter the standings to a single division and you'll see its **zones**:

- the **promotion zone** at the top (not shown for the top tier, since there is nowhere higher to go)
- the **relegation zone** at the bottom, which only the GMs see unless the league decides to show it to everyone

The zones are a guide to the table, not an automatic move.

## A fresh start after a move

Every time your division changes, whether it's a call-up, a promotion, a relegation, a transfer or a GM moving you, you get a clean slate in your new division. Say you're promoted from Mid Card to Heavyweight:

- **Your goals restart.** Your promotion goals, the **Stay sharp** checks and the streaks behind them count only matches from the moment you joined Heavyweight. A 5-match win streak from Mid Card doesn't carry over, and neither does a rough patch.
- **The division table starts you at 0 points.** When the standings are filtered to Heavyweight, your league points, record, Last 5 and streak count only matches since you joined, so you start at the bottom with 0 points and 0-0-0 and climb from there. See [How Standings Work](how-standings-work). The table shows "Records count from when each wrestler joined this division." as a reminder.
- **Title contender rankings start fresh too.** For a title that belongs to Heavyweight, only your matches since you joined count towards the contender rankings. For a tag title, a team counts from when its second member joined.
- **Your record never changes.** Your career win-loss-draw record, your all-time points on your profile and the **All** divisions view of the standings keep every match you've ever had, and so do the season standings. A move only changes what counts from here on.

Matches count from when their result was recorded, not the date they were booked for. So a match booked before your move but played after it counts in your new division.

### "Counting since" on your progress cards

Your progress cards on your profile, your dashboard and your division page show where the count starts, for example *Counting since your promotion to Heavyweight on Mar 3, 2026* or *Counting since your goals were reset on Apr 12, 2026*. The info icon next to it is a reminder that your record still includes every match; only the goals restarted.

### When a GM resets your goals

A GM can also give you a fresh start without moving you, for example after a long break or a storyline restart. A goals reset:

- restarts your goals and the streaks behind them from 0% at that moment
- removes any promotion watch flag, so the GMs can look at you again with fresh eyes
- leaves your division, your record, your season standings, the division table and your title contender rankings exactly as they were

You get a notification when it happens, and the GM may add a short note about why.

## The GM makes the final call

Nothing moves you automatically, not the meter, not the zones. A GM always decides, usually at the end of a season, and storylines and roster balance matter as much as numbers.

When you move:

- your division changes and you get a notification
- your goals start fresh against the goals of your new division (see **A fresh start after a move** above)
- a promotion appears in the activity feed, and the GM may announce it on the News page
- a relegation is shown neutrally, as "*name* moves to *division*", and only appears in the activity feed if the GM chose to announce it

Your profile keeps a **Division History** of every move, for example *Young Boys → Jobber → Mid Card*.

## For league staff

This section is for GMs and Moderators.

- **Tiers and zone sizes**: set them in **Admin → Divisions**. Setting a tier creates default promotion and relegation tracks for the divisions that have one above or below them. Zone sizes default to 2 places; 0 turns a zone off.
- **Tracks and goals**: edit them in **Admin → Call-Ups**. A track belongs to the division wrestlers move out of. The target division comes from the tiers when you move someone.
- **Flags**: the **Division movement** tab ranks each division's candidates. Promotion watch is public. Relegation watch is for staff only, and the wrestler never sees their own relegation flag.
- **Showing relegation publicly**: the **Show relegation watch to everyone** switch in **Admin → Feature Management → Ticker Settings** shows relegation zones in the standings, relegation meters on profiles and relegation watch in the activity feed to everyone. It is off by default. Even when it's on, a wrestler never sees their own relegation meter or flag.
- **Season review**: when a season is completed, a snapshot of every player's promotion and relegation standing is taken. Each division is ranked by league points over that season, counting only results since each wrestler joined it, the same as the division table on the Standings page. Open it in **Admin → Call-Ups → Season review**, adjust the recommended decisions, and apply them. **Announce** is unticked by default, so moves are quiet unless you tick it.
- **Resetting goals**: in the candidate list, **Reset goals** on a player's row restarts their goals on every track out of their division. Add an optional note; it shows in the history and the player is notified. Their record, the division table and contender rankings aren't affected.
- **New relegation goals**: besides the defaults, a relegation track can use **Losses** (matches lost since the last move) and **Winless streak** (matches in a row without a win). Losses in Hot or Scorching rivalries never count for either.
- **Executing moves**: a promotion or relegation always goes one rung. If several divisions share the next tier, pick the target. Moving a champion out of a division-locked title's division asks you to confirm first.
