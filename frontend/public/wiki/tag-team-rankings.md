# How Tag Team Rankings Work

Tag titles rank **tag teams**, not individual players. Open the Contenders page in the **Rankings** section and pick a tag title from the dropdown: each row is a team, with its name, both members and its stats. The score itself works the same way as for singles titles; see [Contender Rankings](/guide/wiki/contenders).

## Which Teams Can Be Ranked

A team appears in a tag title's rankings only when all of these are true:

- **The team is active.** Teams still waiting for a partner to accept or for staff approval aren't ranked, and neither are dissolved teams.
- **Both members can be ranked.** Each member must be an active, visible player with a wrestler.
- **Both members are in the title's division.** If the tag title belongs to a division, both partners need to be in it. One member in the division isn't enough.
- **The team has played enough.** A team needs at least the minimum number of tag matches in the active season (3 unless staff have changed it). A title lists up to 10 teams.
- **The team doesn't hold the title.** The current champions are shown at the top and aren't ranked.

## Which Matches Count

Only completed tag matches in the active season count, and only for registered teams.

- A match counts for a team when the two players on one side form that team **on the match date**. Matches the pair wrestled before the team was created, or after it was dissolved, don't count for it.
- **Random pairings don't count.** If two players who aren't a registered team wrestle together, that match gives no points to any team. It still counts towards each player's own singles record.
- Triple threat and fatal four-way tag matches are supported: every side that is a registered team gets the result.
- When a team beats another registered team, the loser's record feeds the winner's quality-of-wins score.

## Staff Overrides on Tag Titles

Staff can bump a team to #1 or send it to the bottom for storyline or disciplinary reasons. On a tag title an override always targets a **team**, never a single player. The same rules apply as for the rankings: the team must be active, both members must be eligible and in the title's division, and the current champions can't be overridden. If a team dissolves, its override stops showing because the team is no longer ranked.

## Your Team's Status

**My Contender Status** has a **Tag Titles** section that shows where your active team stands on each tag title, how many tag matches it has played this period, and how many it needs to qualify. Your team's page also shows its current rank as a chip.

## Title Reigns Belong to Teams

When a registered team wins a tag title, the reign is linked to that team:

- The Championships page shows the team's name as the champion.
- The team's page lists every title it has held in a **Title Reigns** table.
- If the two champions aren't a registered team (a random pairing), the reign has no team. It is still recorded for both players.
- If staff delete the match that changed the title, the previous reign is restored together with its team link.

## Creating and Editing Teams

Players form teams by inviting a partner, and staff approve the request. Staff can also create a team directly from **Manage Tag Teams** with **Create Tag Team**: the team is active at once and any pending requests involving either player are closed. Staff can rename a team or change its image at any time, and the new name shows on every page, including past reigns.

A player can only be in one active team at a time, and two active teams can't share a name.
