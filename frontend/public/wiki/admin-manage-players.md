# Managing Players

The **Manage Players** page lets you edit and remove players. Staff don't add players here: a player record is created automatically when someone signs up.

Search by name, wrestler or PSN ID, and use the filter to show one division or players with **No Division**.

## Hidden Players

A player with no wrestler name is **hidden**. They can sign in and use their account, but they don't appear on the roster or standings, and you can't pick them when you book a match or build a tournament.

Hidden players stay out of this table too. When there are any, a **Show hidden** checkbox appears with the count. Tick it to list them; they show a **Hidden** badge in the Wrestler column.

To bring a hidden player back:

- The player enters a wrestler name themselves. The site prompts them to do this when they sign in, and they appear as soon as they save.
- Or staff assign a wrestler, either here under **Edit** or in the **Assigned Wrestler** column of [User Management](admin-user-management).

## Strikes

The **Discipline** button on each row opens the player's strike record, and the status column shows a badge while they have active strikes. Deactivated players are hidden from this table until you tick **Show hidden**. See [Strikes](admin-strikes).

## Editing a Player

1. Find the player in the table
2. Click **Edit**
3. Change the player name, PSN ID, wrestler, alternate wrestler, division, alignment, video upload permission or image
4. Click **Update Player** to save

The wrestler lists come from **Manage Wrestlers**, so add a wrestler there first if they're missing. Setting the wrestler back to **Needs Wrestler — assign later** hides the player from public pages; the form warns you before you save.

## Deleting a Player

1. Find the player in the table
2. Click **Delete**
3. Confirm the action in the dialog

**Note:** You cannot delete a player who is currently a champion. Vacate the title under **Championships** first, or let them lose it in a title match. You also cannot delete a player with active strikes or a deactivated account. See [Strikes](admin-strikes).

Deleting removes the player from the roster and from their faction and tag team, and frees their wrestlers. A tag team they were in is dissolved. Their season standings rows are removed.

Their past matches, title reigns and tournament results stay in the history. Where the site has no wrestler name saved for them, they show as **Former member**. The same person can sign up again later, and they start as a new player.

## Image Upload Guidelines

- Supported formats: JPEG, PNG, GIF, WebP
- Maximum file size: 5MB
- Images show on the player's pages and in standings
