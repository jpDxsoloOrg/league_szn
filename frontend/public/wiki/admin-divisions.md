# Managing Divisions

The **Divisions** page lets you group players by weight class, card position, or role, for example the Heavyweight, Mid-Card and Jobber divisions in the [League Rules](league-rules). Divisions decide who can contend for a title that is locked to a division.

## Creating a Division

1. Go to **Divisions** and click **Create Division**
2. Fill in the division details:
   - **Division Name** — e.g., "Heavyweight", "Mid-Card", "Jobber"
   - **Description** (optional) — e.g., "Top-tier stars" or "Enhancement talent"
3. Click **Create Division** to save

## Editing a Division

1. Find the division in the table
2. Click **Edit**
3. Update the name or description
4. Click **Update Division** to confirm changes

## Deleting a Division

1. Find the division in the table
2. Click **Delete**
3. Confirm the action in the dialog

**Note:** You cannot delete a division that has players assigned to it, or one that a championship is locked to. Move the players and change the championship first.

## Assigning Players to Divisions

Division moves are made by staff. There are two places to do it:

- In **Manage Players**, click **Edit** on a player, choose a **Division** or **No Division**, and click **Update Player**
- In **User Management**, use the **Division** dropdown on a row, which saves straight away

New sign-ups go into a division named "Young Boys" if your league has one. Otherwise they start with no division.

Division transfer requests are switched off, so players can't ask for a move on the site and the **Division Transfers** page doesn't show in the Admin menu.
