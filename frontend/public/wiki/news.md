# News and the League Wire

The league office posts articles and announcements on the **News** page. The **League Wire** is the moving strip along the bottom of the screen that keeps tonight's shows, new champions, headlines and the latest results in view.

## News

Open **Home**, then **News**.

- **Top story** — The newest story leads the page with its opening lines. Choose **Read the story** to open it in full.
- **More stories** — Older stories follow as cards, newest first. Choose a headline to read it.
- **Breaking** — Stories the league office marked as urgent carry a **Breaking** tag.
- A story can include pictures and a video.

A story stays on News for as long as the league office keeps it published. When a story is taken down or reaches its end date, it leaves the page and its link shows **Story not found**.

Some stories also appear as a pop-up when signed-in members open the site. The league office chooses which ones. **Don't show again** hides that story's pop-up. It stays on the News page.

## The League Wire

The League Wire runs along the bottom of the screen on every page except Admin and the sign-in pages. On a phone it sits just above the tab bar and stays in place while you scroll.

It shows these, in this order. A part with nothing to report is skipped, and the GMs choose which parts run, so you won't always see all of them.

1. **Breaking** — Important messages from the GMs.
2. **Tonight** — Shows on today or running now, and how many days until the next PPV.
3. **New champion** — Titles that changed hands in the last week.
4. **Call-up watch** — Wrestlers the GMs are considering for a call-up. See [Call-Ups](call-ups).
5. **Promotion watch** — Wrestlers the GMs are watching for a promotion to a higher division. See [Promotion and Relegation](division-movement).
6. **Hot rivalry** — The league's hottest rivalries.
7. **GM update** — Other messages from the GMs.
8. **News** — The latest headlines from the News page, newest first.
9. **Upcoming** — The next shows with their dates.
10. **Champions** — Each current champion with the title they hold.
11. **Results** — The last completed show, followed by its matches in card order.

Choose a headline to open it: the show, the story, the player, the rivalry or the championships. A GM message can also link to another website, which opens in a new tab.

### Pausing or hiding it

- Hold the pointer over the strip, or press the pause button at its right end. It also stops while a headline has keyboard focus.
- Press **×** to hide it. It stays hidden until you close the tab.
- If your device is set to reduce motion, nothing scrolls. One headline at a time fades in instead, a new one every few seconds.

The League Wire updates every couple of minutes, so a result can take a short while to appear.

## For league staff

Stories are the announcements you publish in **Admin**, under **Announcements**, either with the **GM Wizard** (Match Card, Breaking News or Video / Poster) or with **Create Announcement**. Anything you publish there appears on News and in the League Wire.

- **Pop-ups are opt-in.** On the wizard's last step, tick **Also show as a pop-up when members open the site** to put the story in front of signed-in members as well. It is off by default, so a story goes to News only unless you tick it.
- To change it later, choose **Edit** on the story in **Announcements** and tick or clear **Also show as a pop-up**. The **Pop-up** column in the list shows which stories pop up and which are **News only**.
- Stories published before this option existed are News only.

- Set the priority to **High** to tag a story **Breaking**. High is the default, so lower it for routine posts.
- Switch a story off, or give it an end date, to take it off News.
- Show results reach the League Wire once the show is marked completed.

### Ticker messages and settings

- **Ticker messages.** In **Admin**, under **Announcements**, the **Ticker** section holds short GM messages for the League Wire. Each one has a start and an expiry and only runs between them. Add a link if you like: a site path such as `/events`, or a full `https://` address.
- **Pinned messages.** Tick **Pin to the front of the ticker** to run a message under **Breaking** at the very front. Unpinned messages run as **GM update**.
- **Expire now** takes a message off the League Wire straight away.
- In the **GM Wizard**, tick **Also post to ticker** to post a ticker message along with the announcement.
- **Ticker settings.** In **Admin**, under **Feature Management**, switch **League Ticker** off to remove the strip for everyone. **Ticker Settings** on the same page choose which parts run and the scroll speed: **Slow**, **Normal** or **Fast**. The speed has no effect for people who have reduced motion on.
