# News and the League Wire

The league office posts articles and announcements on the **News** page. The **League Wire** is the moving strip along the bottom of the screen that keeps the latest shows, results and headlines in view.

## News

Open **Home**, then **News**.

- **Top story** — The newest story leads the page with its opening lines. Choose **Read the story** to open it in full.
- **More stories** — Older stories follow as cards, newest first. Choose a headline to read it.
- **Breaking** — Stories the league office marked as urgent carry a **Breaking** tag.
- A story can include pictures and a video.

A story stays on News for as long as the league office keeps it published. When a story is taken down or reaches its end date, it leaves the page and its link shows **Story not found**.

Signed-in members also see each new story once as a pop-up when they open the site. **Don't show again** hides that story's pop-up. It stays on the News page.

## The League Wire

The League Wire runs along the bottom of every page except Admin. On a phone it sits just above the bottom bar. It shows, in this order:

1. **Upcoming shows** — The next shows with their dates. A show that is running now is tagged **Live**.
2. **News** — The latest headlines from the News page.
3. **Results** — Match results from the most recent show, then from the shows before it in the Monday, Wednesday, Friday rotation, newest first. Each show's name and date appear once, tagged **Results**, followed by that show's matches in card order. Title matches are tagged in gold with the title's name.

Choose a headline to open the show, the story or the show's results.

### Pausing it

- Hold the pointer over the strip, or press the pause button at its right end.
- If your device is set to reduce motion, the strip doesn't move. Swipe or scroll it sideways to read on.

The League Wire updates every few minutes, so a result can take a short while to appear.

## For league staff

Stories are the announcements you publish in **Admin**, under **Announcements**. Anything you publish there appears on News and in the League Wire.

- Set the priority to **High** to tag a story **Breaking**. High is the default, so lower it for routine posts.
- Switch a story off, or give it an end date, to take it off News.
- Show results reach the League Wire once the show is marked completed.
