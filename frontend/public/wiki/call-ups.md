# Call-Ups: From Young Boys to the Main Roster

Every new wrestler starts out in the **Young Boys** division. It is the league's training ground: a place to find your feet, learn the ropes and show the GMs what you can do. The **Road to the Main Roster** bar on your profile and your dashboard shows how close you are to earning a call-up.

## Your goals

The call-up track has four goals. Each one counts for a quarter of the bar, so you can make progress in any order and every bit of effort shows.

| Goal | What it means |
|------|---------------|
| **Compete in 10 matches** | Any completed match you take part in counts, win or lose. |
| **Average at least 3 stars** | The average user star rating of your matches. It only kicks in once at least 5 of your matches have been rated, so ask people to rate your matches! |
| **Cut 3 promos that get a reaction** | A promo counts once at least 3 reactions from other people land on it. |
| **Win 5 matches** | Wins in completed matches. |

The bar fills a little for each goal as you get closer to it, not only when you finish it. Halfway to 10 matches already moves your bar. A goal turns green with a check mark once you have met it.

Only you and the GMs can see the goal-by-goal checklist. Everyone else sees just your overall percentage.

## What counts, and what doesn't

- **Self-reactions don't count.** Reacting to your own promo won't move your bar. Only reactions from other users count towards your promo goal.
- **Hidden promos don't count.** If a GM hides a promo, it drops out of your progress.
- **Only completed matches count.** A scheduled match adds to your bar once a GM records the result.
- **Only your time in Young Boys counts.** Progress is measured from the day you joined the division. Matches and promos from before that don't count, so everyone starts from the same line.

Progress updates automatically a few moments after a result is recorded, a match is rated or a promo gets a reaction. If your bar looks behind, give it a minute and refresh.

## Milestones

You'll get a notification when you:

- complete one of your goals
- pass **50%**, **75%** and **100%** of the bar

Each milestone is announced once. If your average rating dips and then recovers, you won't get the same notification twice. When you reach 100%, the GMs are told too.

## Under consideration

When a GM thinks you're getting close, they can mark you as **under consideration** for a call-up. A banner then appears on your profile and a small badge shows next to your name in the standings, so the whole league knows you're one to watch. You'll get a notification, and it shows up in the activity feed.

Being under consideration is a compliment, not a promise. It means the GMs are watching you closely.

## The GM makes the final call

Hitting 100% does **not** move you automatically, and you don't need to be at 100% to be called up. The bar is a guide. A GM always makes the final decision and picks which main-roster division you join. Storylines, roster balance and how you carry yourself in the locker room matter too.

When you're called up, your division changes, you get a notification, and the move appears in the activity feed. The GM may also announce it on the News page.

## Tips

- Keep competing. Every match moves the bar, even a loss.
- Put effort into your promos and share them so other people react.
- Ask your opponents and friends to rate your matches.
- Enjoy it. Everyone on the main roster started where you are now.
