# Scheduling Matches

The **Schedule Match** page lets you create upcoming matches. To add a match to an event, you can also click **Add Match** on the event page. That opens this same form with the event already chosen and returns you to the event when you save.

## Creating a Match

1. Go to **Schedule Match**
2. Under **Match**, fill in:
   - **Match Format** — Choose from the types you've configured (see Match Config). Examples: Singles, Tag Team, Triple Threat, Fatal 4-Way, Battle Royal.
   - **Stipulation** (optional) — Choose from the stipulations you've configured, or leave it as a standard match. Examples: Ladder, Steel Cage, Hell in a Cell, Last Man Standing.
   - **Championship Match** (optional) — Tick it and select the title on the line. If the title is locked to a division, everyone in the match must be in that division.
3. Under **Placement**, fill in:
   - **Season** — Starts on the active season. Pick **No Season (Exhibition)** if the result shouldn't count towards season standings.
   - **Event** (optional) — Pick an upcoming or in-progress event, or leave it as a standalone match. If you pick an event, also choose the **Card Position**.
   - **Tournament** (optional) — Pick a tournament that hasn't finished.
4. Under **Competitors**, pick at least 2 players. You can filter them by division. Tag team formats ask for teams instead (see below).
5. Click **Schedule Match** to save

There is no date field. The match takes the date of its event, or today's date if it is standalone.

Hidden players, who have no wrestler name, aren't offered in the player lists. If you picked an event, the lists lead with the people who checked in for it.

## Tag Team Matches

A format with "tag" in its name asks for teams. Build at least 2 teams of 2 or more, either from **Use Tag Teams** or with **Pick Individuals**. **Add Team** adds another side.

## Open Match for Signups

For any format except tag team, you can tick **Open match for signups** and set the **Number of spots**. Spots can be left open for wrestlers to claim, filled by you, or filled and locked. You can't record a result until every spot is filled.

## Pre-fill from challenge or promo

You can open Schedule Match from **Promos**:

- On the **Call-outs** tab, click **Schedule** on an accepted challenge
- On the **Promos** tab, click **Schedule Match** on a call-out promo that names a target

Participants and the match type are filled in for you, and a challenge also fills in its stipulation. You can change them before saving. A challenge for a tag team match needs its teams picked by hand, and a title match needs **Championship Match** ticked and the title chosen.

Saving marks the challenge as scheduled, or hides the promo from the public feed. The match keeps a link to it.

## Rivalries

If everyone in an active rivalry is in the match and only one rivalry fits, the match is linked to that rivalry automatically and shows on its **Future Matches** tab.

## Match types and stipulations

Match types and stipulations are not fixed. You add and edit them in **Admin → Match Config**, which has two tabs: **Match Types** and **Stipulations**.

### Match types

Match types define the format (how many competitors, how a winner is determined). Add as many as your league uses. Examples:

- **Singles** — 1 vs 1
- **Tag Team** — 2 vs 2
- **Triple Threat** — 3 participants, first pin/submit wins
- **Fatal 4-Way** — 4 participants, first pin/submit wins
- **Battle Royal** — multiple participants, last one standing wins

### Stipulations

Stipulations are optional rules or gimmicks for a match. Add any your league uses. When scheduling a match, pick one from the list or leave it as a standard match. Examples: Ladder, Steel Cage, Hell in a Cell, Last Man Standing, Tables, Iron Man.
