# Creating Tournaments

The **Tournaments** page in the Admin panel creates new tournaments. Members follow them under **Events → Tournaments**.

## Creating a Tournament

1. Go to **Tournaments**
2. Fill in the tournament details:
   - **Tournament Name** — e.g., "King of the Ring 2025"
   - **Tournament Type** — Single Elimination or Round Robin
   - **Participants** — Select the players competing. Hidden players aren't listed
3. For single elimination, set the **Seed / Matchup Order** with the arrows
4. Click **Create Tournament** to save

## Tournament Types

### Single Elimination

- Needs 4, 8, 16 or another power of two participants
- The bracket is generated for you. The seed order sets the first round: 1 vs 2, 3 vs 4, and so on
- Losers are eliminated
- Winners advance to the next round automatically
- Matches can't end in a draw
- Best for quick tournaments

### Round Robin

- Minimum 2 participants
- Everyone faces everyone
- Points: Win=2, Draw=1, Loss=0
- Most points wins. Ties go to most wins, then to wins in the matches between the tied players
- The tournament finishes when every pairing has been played
- Best for league-style play

## Managing Tournament Matches

Creating a tournament doesn't create its matches. You schedule each one yourself.

1. Schedule the match via **Schedule Match**
2. Select the tournament from the dropdown. Finished tournaments aren't offered
3. For single elimination, book the two players the bracket has paired, or the result won't move the bracket on
4. Record results as matches are completed
5. Standings and brackets update automatically
