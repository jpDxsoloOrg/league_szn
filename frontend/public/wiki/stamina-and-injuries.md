# Stamina and Injuries

Every match takes something out of you. Your **stamina** goes down with each match you work, comes back once a week, and the more tired you are, the more likely you are to get hurt. An injury keeps you out of new matches until your return date.

The GMs switch this on for the league. Until they do, none of it applies: matches don't cost stamina and nobody gets injured.

## Stamina

Everyone starts with full stamina, whether you're in Heavyweight, Mid Card, Jobber or Young Boys. Every match you're in uses some of it, whether the GMs record the result by hand or settle it with the match sim. Wins, losses and draws all cost the same.

How much a match takes depends on:

- **The kind of match.** A standard match takes the least. Ladders, cages, hardcore matches and technical matches take more, and an Iron Man or Last Man Standing match takes the most. It follows the match's sim category (see [Wrestler Builds](wrestler-builds)).
- **The format.** In a tag match, a multi-person match or a battle royal you share the work, so you lose less than in a singles match.
- **Your Endurance.** The higher the Endurance stat in your build, the less stamina each match costs you. It's the stat to raise if you want to work a lot of matches.

The GMs set the exact numbers and can change them, so this page describes how it works rather than the figures.

### Sunday recovery

Stamina doesn't come back between matches. It comes back once a week, at **midnight on Sunday in the league's time zone**. Usually that means back to full; the GMs can also set it to give back a fixed amount each week. A busy week of matches can leave you running on empty until Sunday.

### Where to see it

Your stamina is **private**: only you and the GMs see the number. Your dashboard and your own profile show a stamina meter with:

- your stamina right now
- when it recovers, in your own time zone
- how much your next booked match will cost, and what you'll have left after it

## Injuries

After every match, each wrestler in it rolls for an injury. The chance depends on your stamina after the match: when you're fresh it's small, and it climbs as you get tired. Riskier matches, like hardcore, cage and ladder matches, raise it a bit more.

### How bad and how long

An injury is **Minor**, **Moderate** or **Major**. Minor injuries are the most common and the shortest; major ones are rare and keep you out the longest. Getting hurt while you're exhausted makes a bad injury more likely.

Every injury has a **return date**, and it always falls on a weekly reset: midnight on a Sunday, league time. You're out for at least the number of weeks the injury lasts. On your return date you're cleared to compete and get a notification saying so.

You get a notification when you're injured too, with the match it happened in and your return date.

### What an injury stops

While you're injured you **can't be booked into new matches**. That means:

- you can't claim a slot in a match
- you can't issue or accept challenges
- the GMs can't add you to a new match or tournament without a deliberate override

Matches you were already booked in stay on the card. The GMs decide what happens to them; they see a warning on each one. If the GMs book you anyway for a storyline, you're at a disadvantage when that match is simulated.

### What everyone sees

Injuries are **public**. While you're out, an **Injured, returns {date}** badge shows next to your name on your profile, in the standings, on the Free Agents page and on match cards. Your profile also lists your **injury history**: each injury's severity, how many weeks it lasted and the match it came from. Your stamina number stays private.

## When the GMs step in

The GMs can heal an injury early, make one longer or shorter, put a storyline injury on you, or adjust your stamina. You get a notification each time. If a match result is deleted, the stamina it cost comes back and an injury from that match is removed, unless a GM has already changed it.

## In the match sim

Tiredness shows in [simulated matches](wrestler-builds). The lower your stamina, the lower your rating, and an injured wrestler who is booked anyway is rated lower still. The play-by-play may call it too: a wrestler who looks gassed, or one selling an injury.

## Shop items

If the league economy is on, the [shop](contracts-and-money) sells items that help:

| Item | What it does |
|------|--------------|
| **Injury shield** | Lowers your injury chance for your next few matches. You can only have one active at a time. |
| **Rehab** | Takes time off an active injury. Your return date moves earlier but stays on a Sunday, and an injury never ends sooner than a week after it started. You need at least two weeks left to buy it. |
| **Stamina top-up** | Gives you stamina back straight away, up to your maximum. It doesn't change your Sunday recovery. |
| **Sim interference** | Boosts your rating in one of your scheduled matches when the GMs simulate it. If they record that match by hand instead, you get your money back. |

## Good to know

- Stamina and injuries are the same for everyone, in every division.
- A storyline injury works like any other: you can't be booked, and it ends on its return date unless a GM changes it.
- Stamina only drops when a result is recorded. Being booked in a match doesn't cost anything until it's played.
