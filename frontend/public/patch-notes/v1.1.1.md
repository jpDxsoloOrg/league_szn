A quick follow-up to 1.1.0. Players up for a call-up are now easier to spot, and the League Wire stays focused on the latest show.

## Call-Up Watch Fixes

- **The badge now shows on the podium.** A Young Boy marked as under consideration gets the "Call-up watch" badge in the top-three cards on the standings page, not just in the table below. The players closest to a call-up are usually the ones at the top, so this is where you'll see it most.
- **Call-ups on the League Wire.** Every player the GMs are considering for a call-up now has their own "Call-up watch" headline on the ticker. Choose it to open their profile.

**Read more:** [Call-Ups: Young Boys to the Main Roster](/guide/wiki/call-ups)

## League Wire

- **Results from the last show only.** The Results section now shows the matches from the most recent completed show instead of the last few shows. The ticker stays short, so the newest results come around sooner.
