Wrestling takes it out of you. Every match now costs stamina, stamina comes back on Sunday, and the more tired you are, the more likely you are to get hurt. An injury keeps you off new cards until your return date. The GMs switch stamina and injuries on for the league, and they tune how it all works. Here's everything that's new.

## Stamina

- **Every match costs stamina.** Whether the GMs record the result by hand or settle it with the match sim, everyone in the match loses some stamina. Wins, losses and draws cost the same.
- **The kind of match matters.** A standard match takes the least. Ladders, cages, hardcore and technical matches take more, and an Iron Man or Last Man Standing match takes the most. Tag matches, multi-person matches and battle royals take less than singles, because you share the work.
- **Endurance pays off.** The higher the Endurance stat in your build, the less each match costs you.
- **Sunday recovery.** Stamina comes back once a week, at midnight on Sunday in the league's time zone. A busy week can leave you running on empty until then.
- **Your stamina is private.** Only you and the GMs see the number. Your dashboard and your profile show a stamina meter with your next recovery, in your own time zone, and what your next booked match will cost.

## Injuries

- **Tired wrestlers get hurt.** After every match, each wrestler in it rolls for an injury. The chance is small when you're fresh and climbs as your stamina drops. Riskier matches, like hardcore, cage and ladder matches, raise it a bit more.
- **Minor, Moderate or Major.** Minor injuries are the most common and the shortest. Getting hurt while you're exhausted makes a bad one more likely.
- **Return dates land on Sunday.** Every injury ends at a weekly reset. You get a notification when you're hurt, with your return date, and another when you're cleared to compete.
- **Injured means off the card.** While you're out you can't be booked into new matches, claim a slot or issue or accept challenges. Matches you were already booked in stay put, and the GMs decide what happens to them.
- **Public badge, public history.** An **Injured, returns {date}** badge shows next to your name on your profile, in the standings, on the Free Agents page and on match cards. Your profile lists your injury history: severity, weeks out and the match it came from.

## The GMs Run It

- **Switched on by the GMs.** Stamina and injuries are off until the GMs turn them on for the league. They also set how much matches drain, how likely injuries are and how long they last.
- **Heal, extend and storylines.** The GMs can heal an injury early, make one longer or shorter, put a storyline injury on a wrestler, or adjust stamina. You get a notification each time.
- **Booking an injured wrestler is a choice.** The GMs can still book you while you're injured, for a storyline, but only on purpose. You can never get around it yourself.
- **Corrections put things back.** If a match result is deleted, the stamina it cost comes back and an injury from it is removed.

## The Shop's Injury and Sim Items Work Now

- **Injury shield** lowers your injury chance for your next few matches.
- **Rehab** takes time off an active injury. Your return date moves earlier and still lands on a Sunday.
- **Stamina top-up** gives you stamina back straight away, up to your maximum.
- **Sim interference** now boosts your rating when the GMs simulate the match you bought it for. If they record that match by hand instead, you still get your money back.

## Stamina in the Match Sim

- **Tired wrestlers are rated lower.** In a simulated match, the lower your stamina, the lower your rating. A wrestler booked while injured is rated lower still.
- **The play-by-play notices.** A wrestler who's running on empty can look gassed, and an injured one can be seen selling the injury.
