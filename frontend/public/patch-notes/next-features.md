Here's what we're building next. Plans can change as we go, but this is where we're headed.

## Promotion & Relegation

Divisions are getting a ladder. Climb it.

- **Division tiers.** Divisions get an order from the top of the card down, with the Young Boys as the developmental base.
- **Promotion tracks.** Just like call-ups, every division gets clear goals for moving up: win rate, match ratings, contender rankings and Match of the Night honours. You'll see your promotion progress on your profile.
- **Promotion watch.** When the GMs think you're ready for the next level, you'll get a "Promotion watch" banner and badge, and a notification.
- **Standings zones.** Filter the standings to a division and the promotion zone is highlighted at the top of the table, league-table style.
- **End-of-season review.** When a season ends, the GMs get a snapshot of where everyone stands and can make all of the moves in one go.
- **Division history.** Your profile will show your journey through the divisions, like "Young Boys → NXT → Raw", with dates.
- **Stay sharp guidance.** On your own profile you'll see a few friendly pointers if your form dips. Losses in a hot rivalry storyline never count against you.

## League Wire Upgrades

The ticker at the bottom of the screen is getting smarter.

- **More live segments.** Potential call-ups, players on promotion watch, new champions, tonight's shows, the countdown to the next PPV, and the hottest rivalries.
- **Messages from the GMs.** GMs can post short ticker messages, like "Draft night this Friday!", that run until they expire. Breaking News announcements can go straight to the ticker too.
- **Easier to read.** It pauses when you hover over it, uses a gentle fade instead of scrolling if your device is set to reduce motion, and you can hide it for the rest of your visit.
- **League settings.** The GMs can choose which segments run and turn the ticker on or off.
