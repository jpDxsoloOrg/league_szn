The main roster is now a ladder you can climb, and the League Wire has moved to the top of the page with a lot more to say. Here's everything that's new.

## Promotion & Relegation

Getting called up out of the Young Boys is only the start. Divisions now sit on a ladder, and the GMs move wrestlers up and down it as the seasons go by.

### The division ladder

- **Division tiers.** Main-roster divisions now have a place on the ladder. Tier 1 is the top, and divisions on the same tier are equals, so Raw and SmackDown can sit side by side with NXT below them. The Young Boys and other developmental divisions sit below every tier.
- **One rung at a time.** A promotion moves you up one rung, for example from NXT to Raw. A move down is one rung too.

### Your road up

- **Promotion meter.** If there's a division above yours, your profile has a new gold **promotion meter** under **Division Movement**. It works like the call-up bar and moves a little as you get closer to each goal, not only when you finish it.
- **Promotion goals.** By default the GMs look for:
  - winning 60% of your last 8 matches
  - an average of at least 3.25 stars
  - a top-3 spot in the contender rankings for a title in your division
  - a Match of the Night
- **Your checklist is yours.** Everyone can see your meter's overall percentage. Only you and the GMs see the goal-by-goal checklist.
- **Promotion watch.** When a GM thinks you're getting close, they can put you on promotion watch. You get a notification and a banner on your profile, your name runs on the League Wire under **Promotion watch**, and the whole league sees it in the activity feed.

### Stay sharp

- **Friendly tips, not warnings.** If your form dips, your profile shows a **Stay sharp** card with a tip or two, like chasing a few more wins or asking for a match. Follow the tips and you're in good shape.
- **Storyline losses never count against you.** If a match belongs to a Hot or Scorching rivalry, a loss in it is simply ignored when the GMs look at form. So if you're putting someone over in a red-hot feud, lose with pride.

### Standings, moves and history

- **Standings zones.** Filter the standings to a single division and the promotion zone is highlighted at the top of the table. The zones are a guide, not an automatic move.
- **The GMs make the final call.** Nothing moves you automatically. A GM always decides, usually at the end of a season, and storylines and roster balance matter as much as the numbers.
- **Moves are announced neutrally.** When you change divisions you get a notification, and your progress starts fresh against your new division's goals. A promotion shows up in the activity feed and can be announced on the News page. Other moves are shown simply as "*name* moves to *division*".
- **Division history.** Your profile now keeps a record of every move you've made, like *Young Boys → NXT → Raw*.
- **End-of-season review for GMs.** When a season ends, the GMs get a snapshot of where everyone stands, with recommended moves they can adjust and then apply in one go.

## League Wire Upgrades

The League Wire, the moving news strip, now runs just under the top bar on every page instead of at the bottom of the screen. On a phone it sits under the header and stays put while you scroll.

- **More live segments.** In order: Breaking, Tonight (shows on today plus the countdown to the next PPV), New champion, Call-up watch, Promotion watch, Hot rivalry, GM update, News (newest first), Upcoming shows, Champions and the latest Results. A segment with nothing to report is skipped.
- **Messages from the GMs.** GMs can now post their own short ticker messages that run until they expire. Pinned ones lead the ticker as **Breaking**, the rest run as **GM update**. A Breaking News announcement can go straight to the ticker too.
- **Click through.** Choose a headline to open the show, the story, the player, the rivalry or the championships.
- **Easier to read.** The ticker pauses when you hover over it, and there's a pause button at its right end. Press **×** to hide it for the rest of your visit. If your device is set to reduce motion, it fades one headline in at a time instead of scrolling.
- **League settings.** The GMs can choose which segments run, set the scroll speed, or turn the ticker off for everyone.
