Your wrestler now has a build: a fighting style, a match specialty, four stats and levels for your signatures and finishers, all bought with points from your division's budget. The GMs also get a match sim that uses those builds to settle a match when it can't be played. Here's everything that's new.

## Build Your Wrestler

- **Points from your division.** Every division has a build budget: 160 points in Heavyweight, 130 in Mid Card, 100 in Jobber and 80 in Young Boys. The meter in the editor shows what you've spent and what's left.
- **Four stats.** Strikes, Grapple, Agility and Endurance all start at 40 and cap at 100. Points up to 75 cost 1 each, up to 90 cost 2, and the last stretch to 100 costs 3, so a maxed stat is a real investment.
- **Pick a style.** Powerhouse, High-flyer, Technician, Brawler, Striker or All-rounder. Each one gives a free stat bonus, and the first five form a circle where each style has the edge over one other and is weak against another. The All-rounder has no edge and no weakness.
- **Choose a match specialty.** High risk, Technical, Endurance, Hardcore or Cage. It's free, but it has to fit your style: a Powerhouse can't specialize in ladder matches. Switch to a style that doesn't allow your specialty and the editor clears it and tells you why.
- **Level up your moves.** Every signature and finisher in your moveset goes from level 1 to 5. A signature level costs 5 points and a finisher level 8. Drop a move from your moveset and you get its points back.

## Your Build on Your Profile

- **The build editor** sits on the Overview tab of your profile. Choose **Set up build** the first time and **Edit build** after that.
- **A build card for everyone to see.** Once you save, your profile shows your style, your specialty, four stat bars with the style bonus marked, and your move levels. Until then the default build stays off your public profile.
- **Dashboard reminders.** Your dashboard tells you when you have points left to spend, when you haven't picked a specialty, or when your build is over budget.

## Builds and Division Moves

- **Your budget follows you.** Move up and you get more points to spend. Your dashboard shows how many.
- **Your build never holds up a relegation.** If your build no longer fits the new budget, you get a notification saying by how much. Tap it to go straight to the build editor.
- **Until you re-spend,** simulated matches use a scaled-down copy of your build that fits the new budget. Your saved build isn't touched, so trim it, save, and you're back to full strength.

## The Match Sim

- **GMs can settle a match with the sim.** When a match can't be played, a GM can let the sim decide it. Only GMs can preview a matchup or simulate a match, and only GMs see the win chances.
- **What goes into it.** Your stats, your move levels, your recent form, the style matchup and whether the match is in your specialty all help. Each kind of match leans on different stats: a ladder match on Agility, a submission match on Grapple, an iron man match on Endurance. None of it guarantees a win, and upsets happen.
- **A simulated result counts.** Records, standings and titles update just like a match that was played.
- **See how it went.** Simulated matches carry a **Simulated** badge on the Matches page and on event cards, and anyone can open the play-by-play, which names your real signatures and finishers.
