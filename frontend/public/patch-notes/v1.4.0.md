The standings now run on league points, so every result counts towards your place in the table and the big moments earn a little extra. Tag teams also get top billing: match cards and results show the team name, not just the two wrestlers. Here's everything that's new.

## League Points in the Standings

- **Every result counts.** A win is worth 3 points, a draw 1 and a loss 0. Winning a Fatal 4-Way is worth the same as a singles win, and in a tag match both winners get 3.
- **Quality wins: +1.** Beat someone who was above you in the table going into the match, or a reigning champion, and you get a bonus point. One per match, and it's yours to keep even if they slump later.
- **Streak bonus: +1.** Every third win in a row adds a bonus point: your 3rd straight win, your 6th, your 9th and so on. A losing run never costs you points.
- **Fair tiebreakers.** Level on points? Strength of schedule comes first, then head-to-head between the tied players, then win %, then wins. Players who haven't wrestled yet sit at the bottom.

## A New Look for the Standings Page

- **A Pts column.** The table is sorted by points, and you can still click any heading to sort by wins, win % or the rest.
- **See where the points came from.** Click or tap a player's points for the breakdown, for example *12 = 9 from results + 2 quality wins + 1 streak bonus*.
- **Podium by points.** The top three are the points leaders, with their points under their names.
- **Ties share a place.** Players who are level on everything share a rank, so a table can read 1, 2, 2, 4.
- **Learn how it works.** A **How standings work** link under the table explains the points, bonuses and tiebreakers with an example.

**Read more:** [Standings](/guide/wiki/standings) · [How Standings Work](/guide/wiki/how-standings-work)

## Points Start Fresh in Your Division

- **Movers start at 0.** Filter the standings to one division and each wrestler's points count only results since they joined it. Promoted from Mid Card to Heavyweight? You start the Heavyweight table at 0 points and climb from there.
- **Your career is safe.** The **All** divisions view and your profile keep every result you've ever had. A move never changes them.
- **Zones still mean the same.** The promotion and relegation zones mark places among the wrestlers who have wrestled in that division.

## Points Everywhere You Look

- **Top of the table on the home page** follows the points order and shows each leader's points.
- **Profiles** show a wrestler's all-time points next to their record, and where they rank, such as **#3 in Young Boys**.
- **The GMs' end-of-season review** now ranks each division by points too, so "top of the division" means the same thing as on the Standings page.

**Read more:** [Dashboard](/guide/wiki/dashboard) · [My Profile](/guide/wiki/profile) · [Promotion and Relegation](/guide/wiki/division-movement)

## Tag Team Names on Match Cards

- **Teams get top billing.** When a side of a match is a registered tag team, the card shows the team name with its members underneath. Results read *The Usos def. The New Day*.
- **Everywhere matches show up.** Event cards and results, the Matches tab, recent results on the dashboard, the main event on the home page, rivalry pages and profiles all use the team name.
- **Search by team.** Type a team's name in the Matches tab to find its matches.
- **Booking by team.** When GMs book a tag match, each side reads **Team 1: The Usos** once it's a registered pair, even when the wrestlers were picked one by one.
