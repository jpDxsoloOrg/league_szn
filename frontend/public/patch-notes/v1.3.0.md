Moving division now means a real fresh start. Your goals, your division's table and that division's title races all count from the day you arrive, while your career record keeps every match you've ever had. Here's everything that's new.

## A Fresh Start After Every Move

Every division change now gives you a clean slate: a call-up, a promotion, a relegation, a transfer or a GM moving you by hand.

- **Your goals restart.** Your promotion goals, the **Stay sharp** checks and the streaks behind them count only matches from the moment you join your new division. Promoted from Mid Card to Heavyweight on a 5-match win streak? That streak stays in Mid Card, and so does any rough patch.
- **Move back, start again.** Each move restarts the count, even if you return to a division you were in before.
- **Your record never changes.** Your career win-loss-draw record and the season standings keep every match. A move only changes what counts from here on.
- **Results count when they're recorded.** A match now counts from the moment its result goes in, not the date it was booked for. A match booked before your move but played after it counts in your new division.

**Read more:** [Promotion and Relegation](/guide/wiki/division-movement) · [Call-Ups: Young Boys to the Main Roster](/guide/wiki/call-ups)

## Division Tables and Title Races Start From Your Move

- **One division, fresh records.** Filter the standings to a single division and each wrestler's record, Last 5 and streak count only matches since they joined it. Someone who has just moved into Heavyweight starts at the bottom at 0-0-0 and climbs from there. A short note under the table explains it.
- **Career record everywhere else.** The **All** view and player profiles still show the full record.
- **Title contender rankings follow your division.** For a title that belongs to a division, only your matches since joining that division count. Move to Mid Card and your ranking for the Mid Card titles builds up from your first match there. For a tag title, a team counts from when its second member joined.

## GMs Can Reset Your Goals

Sometimes a fresh start makes sense without a move, for example after a long break or a storyline restart.

- **Reset goals.** GMs and Moderators can now reset a wrestler's goals from the candidate list. Goals and the streaks behind them restart from 0%, and any promotion watch flag comes off.
- **Nothing else moves.** Your division, record, season standings, the division table and your title contender rankings stay exactly as they were.
- **You'll know about it.** You get a notification, and the GM can add a short note about why. The reset is also kept in the GMs' history.

**Read more:** [Promotion and Relegation](/guide/wiki/division-movement)

## "Counting Since" on Your Progress Cards

- **See where the count starts.** Progress cards on your profile, your dashboard and your division page now show a line like *Counting since your promotion to Heavyweight* or *Counting since your call-up to Jobber*, with the date.
- **The reason, in plain words.** It tells you whether the count started with a call-up, a promotion, a division change or a GM reset.
- **A quick reminder.** The info icon next to it explains that your record still includes every match. Only the goals restarted.

**Read more:** [Promotion and Relegation](/guide/wiki/division-movement) · [Call-Ups: Young Boys to the Main Roster](/guide/wiki/call-ups)

## Two New Stay Sharp Checks

- **More ways to read form.** Besides the existing checks, GMs can now look at how many matches you've lost since your last move and how many you've gone in a row without a win. The **Stay sharp** card has tips for both.
- **Storyline losses still never count.** A loss in a Hot or Scorching rivalry is ignored by both new checks, just like the old ones. Putting someone over in a red-hot feud never counts against you.
