The league has its own money. Sign a contract, get paid for every match, earn a bonus for every win and spend it in the new shop, or gamble it on a favour from the GM. Break the rules and it'll cost you. The economy goes live when the GMs switch it on for the league, so if you don't see it yet, it's on its way. Here's everything that's new.

## Contracts

- **Three kinds of contract.** A **tryout** runs for a few matches, a **match contract** for a set number of matches, and a **season contract** until the season ends.
- **Offers come to you.** When a GM makes you an offer, you get a notification and it waits in your inbox under **My Contract**, with the pay, the length, a note from the GM and how long you have to answer. Accept it, decline it, or let it expire. Accepting one offer withdraws the others.
- **Contracts run their course.** Every match you play counts toward your contract, and it ends when the last match is played. A season contract ends with the season.
- **Free agents.** No contract? You're a free agent, and the new **Free Agents** page lists everyone available to sign. Your profile shows whether you're signed or a free agent.

**Read more:** [Contracts and Money](/guide/wiki/contracts-and-money) · [My Profile](/guide/wiki/profile)

## Getting Paid

- **Pay per match.** Every match you're in pays out once the result is recorded, win, lose or draw.
- **Win bonus.** Win and you get a bonus on top. Free agents still earn the league's free agent win bonus.
- **Sunday payday.** Contracts with a weekly salary pay out every Sunday.
- **Your wallet.** **My Contract** has a **Wallet** tab with your balance and every payment, bonus, fine and purchase. The dashboard has a wallet card with your balance, what you earned this week and when the next payday is. Your money is private: only you and the GMs can see it.

**Read more:** [Contracts and Money](/guide/wiki/contracts-and-money) · [Dashboard](/guide/wiki/dashboard)

## Fines and No-Shows

- **Fines.** GMs can fine you for breaking the rules, like spamming cheesy moves or abusing pause. Every fine comes with a reason you can read in your wallet.
- **No-shows cost you.** Booked for a match and didn't turn up? A GM can mark you as a no-show, which comes with a fine.
- **Debt locks the shop.** If fines push your balance below zero, you're in debt and the shop is locked until you're back to zero. You keep playing and getting paid, so a few wins will sort it out.

**Read more:** [Contracts and Money](/guide/wiki/contracts-and-money)

## The Shop

- **Spend your earnings.** The **Shop** lists what's for sale, with prices set by the GMs.
- **More on the shelves soon.** The injury shield, rehab, stamina top-up and sim interference are already on display, marked **Coming soon**. They go on sale when injuries, stamina and the match sim arrive.
- **What you buy stays private.** Nobody else sees what you bought, including which matches you paid sim interference for.

**Read more:** [Contracts and Money](/guide/wiki/contracts-and-money)

## GM Interference: Feeling Lucky?

- **Ask for anything.** Pay the GM up front and ask for whatever you want: a title shot, a run-in, a bad night for your rival.
- **It's a gamble.** The GM may deliver, deliver part of it, refund you, or keep your money and do nothing at all. You'll find out with a notification.
- **One at a time.** You can have one request waiting on the GM at a time. Your requests and how they turned out are listed in the shop.
