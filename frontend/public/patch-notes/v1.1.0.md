The road to the main roster is now on the map, tag teams finally get the spotlight they deserve, and the League Wire got a polish. Here's everything that's new.

## Young Boys Call-Ups

Every Young Boy now has a clear path to the main roster.

- **Call-up progress bar.** Your profile has a new "Road to the Main Roster" section showing how close you are to a call-up. Your dashboard shows it too.
- **Goal checklist.** On your own profile you can see exactly what the GMs are looking for:
  - compete in 10 matches
  - average 3.0★ or better over at least 5 rated matches
  - cut 3 promos that get at least 3 reactions each
  - win 5 matches
- **It updates by itself.** Every match result, match rating, promo and reaction moves your bar within seconds. Progress counts from the day you joined the Young Boys, and reactions to your own promos don't count.
- **Milestones.** You'll get a notification each time you complete a goal and when you pass 50%, 75% and 100%. Each one is only announced once.
- **Being considered for a call-up.** When the GMs think you're close, they can mark you as under consideration. A banner appears on your profile, a "Call-up watch" badge shows next to your name in the standings, and the whole league sees it in the activity feed.
- **The call-up.** When the GMs pull the trigger, you move to your new division, get a notification, and the move can be announced on the News page.
- **New Call-ups tab in the activity feed** for following who's on the rise.

Hitting 100% doesn't move you automatically, and you don't need 100% to be called up. The GMs always make the final call.

**Read more:** [Call-Ups: Young Boys to the Main Roster](/guide/wiki/call-ups) · [Rate Matches](/guide/wiki/rate-matches) · [Promos](/guide/wiki/promos)

## Tag Team Contender Rankings

- **Tag titles now rank tag teams, not individuals.** The contender rankings for a tag title list active teams, with both members, the team name and team stats.
- **Champions done right.** The champion block for tag titles shows both champions and the team name instead of just one person.
- **Your team's standing.** My Contender Status, My Tag Team and every team page now show where your team ranks for each tag title, and how many matches you still need to qualify.
- **Title reigns belong to teams.** Tag title wins are now linked to the team that won them. The Championships page shows the team name above the champions, and every team page has a Title Reigns history.
- **How it works.** To be ranked, a team needs to be active, have played enough matches together this season, and (for a division title) have both members in that division. Random pairings don't count toward any team.

**Read more:** [How Tag Team Rankings Work](/guide/wiki/tag-team-rankings) · [Contender Rankings](/guide/wiki/contenders) · [Championships](/guide/wiki/championships)

## Tag Team Management for GMs

- **Create a tag team in one step.** GMs can now form a team directly from the admin panel for storyline pairings, without waiting for players to send and accept requests. Players already on an active team are greyed out, and both members are notified.
- **Rename a team or change its image** from the admin panel at any time.
- **No duplicate names.** Two active teams can't share a name.
- **Contender overrides for teams.** GMs can bump or remove a tag team in the tag title rankings, just like they can for singles.

**Read more:** [Admin: Contender Rankings](/guide/wiki/admin-contender-config)

## Divisions

- **Developmental divisions.** Divisions can now be marked as developmental, and one division can be set as the default for new wrestlers. New sign-ups land in the right place even if the division is renamed.

**Read more:** [Divisions](/guide/wiki/divisions) · [Admin: Divisions](/guide/wiki/admin-divisions)

## League Wire and News

- **Results grouped by show**, newest show first.
- **Clickable headlines.** Upcoming shows and news items in the ticker now link straight to the show or article.
- **Champion items are back**, with a belt icon, and the News tag is now gold so it stands out.
- **Every announcement goes to News.** Announcements made in the wizard are always published to the News page. The pop-up is now opt-in, so you only see it for the big ones.

**Read more:** [News and the League Wire](/guide/wiki/news)
